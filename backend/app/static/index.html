The Teesta Basin Context (Sikkim Himalaya)

In October 2023, the sudden outburst of South Lhonak Glacial Lake (~5,200m altitude) cascaded through the steep Lhonak and Zemu Chu gorges, severely overtopping and breaching the 1,200 MW Chungthang Dam (Teesta-III) and damaging bridges and highways down to Singtam and Rangpo. Undercurrent provides an integrated platform linking satellite observations across this entire hydraulic gradient without fragmented data silos.

Mandatory Scientific Distinction (Blueprint Section 7.4):

Satellite optical imagery measures water-spread surface area, which varies seasonally with monsoon inflows. It is NOT a direct measurement of storage volume. Converting surface area to storage volume requires bathymetric surveys and elevation-area-capacity (EAC) curves. Undercurrent intentionally reports water-spread only to maintain scientific defensibility before judges.

Scientific Methodology & Data Provenance

Traceable data lineage and mathematical formulation per Section 5.3 & Section 7 of the Blueprint

Formula 1: Normalized Difference Water Index (NDWI)
NDWI = (Green - NIR) / (Green + NIR)

For Sentinel-2 MSI, Band 3 (Green, 560nm) and Band 8 (NIR, 842nm) at 10m spatial resolution. Water pixels absorb NIR while reflecting green, resulting in positive index values.

Formula 2: Percentage Surface Area Change
% Change = ((A_later - A_earlier) / A_earlier) × 100

Area calculated in UTM Zone 45N (EPSG:32645). A positive delta reflects growth/impoundment; a negative delta reflects seasonal ablation or sudden drainage.

Judge Defense Cheat Sheet (Section 14.4)

Q: Does lake expansion mean a GLOF is imminent?

A: No. Surface expansion indicates meltwater accumulation or moraine impoundment. An outburst requires moraine failure triggers (avalanches, earthquakes, piping). It flags the lake for field inspection.

Q: Can you predict flood arrival time or depth?

A: Not in this prototype. That requires calibrated 2D hydrodynamic modeling (e.g., HEC-RAS or FLO-2D) and breach bathymetry. Undercurrent strictly provides preliminary exposure screening.

Q: Does reservoir area tell you storage volume?

A: No. Optical imagery detects water-spread area only. Converting this to storage volume requires bathymetry or validated elevation-capacity tables.

Q: What is the Indian space-data contribution?

A: ISRO CartoDEM from Bhuvan for elevation/slope profiling and ISRO Bhoonidhi RISAT-1A SAR coherence for penetrating cloud cover during the Oct 2023 monsoon event.
