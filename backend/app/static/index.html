Scientific Distinction: Water-Spread Surface Area vs Storage Volume:

Satellite optical imagery measures water-spread surface area, which fluctuates seasonally with monsoon precipitation and inflow rates. It is NOT a direct volumetric measurement of impounded water storage. Converting surface area to water volume requires surveyed bathymetric contours and elevation-area-capacity (EAC) curves. Undercurrent intentionally reports water-spread only to preserve rigorous scientific defensibility.

Scientific Methodology & Data Lineage

Verifiable data provenance, remote sensing indices, and mathematical formulations

Formula 1: Normalized Difference Water Index (NDWI) McFeeters 1996
NDWI = (Green − NIR) / (Green + NIR)

For Sentinel-2 MSI, Band 3 (Green, 560nm) and Band 8 (NIR, 842nm) at 10m spatial resolution. Pure water pixels strongly absorb NIR radiation while reflecting green wavelengths, yielding positive index values (≥0.14 cutoff).

Formula 2: Percentage Surface Area Change Planar Delta
% Change = ((A_later − A_earlier) / A_earlier) × 100

Area calculated in UTM Zone 45N (EPSG:32645). A positive delta reflects growth or impoundment; a sudden negative delta reflects moraine breach or rapid drainage (-38.70% detected on Oct 04, 2023).
